SCENARIO 01 · IN A HURRY

“I'm running late. I don't want to wait in that ordering line again.”

YOUR BEST RESPONSE

You can order ahead in our app and skip the ordering line.

Lead with convenience. App ordering lets customers order ahead and skip the ordering line.

SCENARIO 02 · FIRST-TIME VISITOR

“Is there a fee to join your rewards program?”

YOUR BEST RESPONSE

It’s free to join! New members can get 50% off a Classic Pearl Milk Tea after downloading the app.

Membership is free. Introduce the new-member Classic Pearl Milk Tea offer.

SCENARIO 03 · REGULAR CUSTOMER

“I buy a drink here almost every day. Is the app worth it?”

YOUR BEST RESPONSE

You earn one point per dollar, and twelve points can get you a $1 reward coupon.

Regular customers benefit from earning points on purchases. 12 points = a $1 reward coupon.

SCENARIO 04 · CURIOUS CUSTOMER

“Where do I find special member deals?”

YOUR BEST RESPONSE

Check the Rewards section in the app for member-only offers.

The Rewards section is the right place to find member-only offers and promotions.

SCENARIO 05 · BIRTHDAY CUSTOMER

“Does being a member give me anything on my birthday?”

YOUR BEST RESPONSE

Members get a birthday bonus of double points.

The supplied program details list birthday bonus points (2× points).

SCENARIO 06 · AFTER PURCHASE

“I already paid. Is there any reason to download the app for next time?”

YOUR BEST RESPONSE

You can order ahead next time and check the Rewards section for offers.

Connect the app to the customer’s next visit: order ahead and discover rewards.

SCENARIO 07 · POINTS QUESTION

“How many points would I earn on a $7 purchase?”

YOUR BEST RESPONSE

7 points.

At one point per $1 spent, a $7 purchase earns 7 points.

SCENARIO 08 · REWARD VALUE

“I have 24 points. What reward coupon value does that represent at the 12-point rate?”

YOUR BEST RESPONSE

$2

24 ÷ 12 = 2, so that is $2 in reward-coupon value at the stated rate.

SCENARIO 09 · APP SKEPTIC

“I don’t want a long sales pitch. What’s the main benefit?”

YOUR BEST RESPONSE

It’s free to join, and you can order ahead and earn rewards.

Keep the pitch short, useful, and accurate. Avoid pressure or unsupported guarantees.

SCENARIO 10 · PROMOTION QUESTION

“Are member-only offers always the same?”

YOUR BEST RESPONSE

There are also special offers and promotions throughout the year.

Member-only offers and special events can change throughout the year.

SCENARIO 11 · ORDER PICKUP

“If I order in the app, can I avoid waiting to place the order in store?”

YOUR BEST RESPONSE

Yes, you can order ahead and skip the ordering line.

Order Ahead skips the ordering line, but does not guarantee instant drink preparation.

SCENARIO 12 · POINTS VALUE

“If I earn 12 points, what can I redeem according to the program?”

YOUR BEST RESPONSE

A $1 reward coupon.

The stated rate is 12 points for a $1 reward coupon.
